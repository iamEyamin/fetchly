A download manager for iPhone

Fetchly downloads files in several parts at once, pauses and resumes them, keeps going when you leave the app, and can start them at a time you choose.

  • Multi-part downloads with pause, resume and automatic retries
  • Saves HLS (.m3u8) streams that are not copy-protected
  • Built-in browser with tabs, private tabs, bookmarks and history
  • Ad and tracker blocking, with a switch for each site
  • Hidden files behind Face ID, checksums, proxy support and a speed limit

Private by design

Fetchly has no ads, no analytics and no account. It collects no data about you. Read the privacy policy for the details.
