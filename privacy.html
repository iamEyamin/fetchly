Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

The short version

Fetchly does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer has no server that receives anything from the app.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in Fetchly is kept on your iPhone and is never sent to the developer:

  • Your downloads and the list of links you downloaded
  • Browser history, bookmarks, open tabs, cookies and website data
  • Your settings, including proxy details and the sites where you turned the ad blocker off
  • Passwords you enter for protected downloads or a proxy, which are stored in the iOS Keychain

You can clear history, cookies and website data in Settings inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Connections the app makes

  • Websites and files you choose. When you browse to a site or download a link, your device connects to that site directly, as any browser does. That site sees your IP address and applies its own privacy policy.
  • Ad-blocking filter lists. Every few days the app downloads public filter lists from their publishers (EasyList, AdGuard and Peter Lowe's list). Those servers see your IP address, as with any download. Nothing about you or your browsing is sent to them.
  • A proxy, if you set one. If you turn on the proxy setting, connections go through the proxy you entered.

Permissions

  • Face ID or passcode unlocks your hidden files. The check is done by iOS; the app never sees your face data or passcode.
  • Notifications tell you when a download finishes. They are created on your device.
  • Clipboard. The app asks iOS whether the clipboard looks like a link so it can offer to download it. It reads the contents only when you tap Paste.

Tips

"Support the developer" tips are optional in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives no payment details and no information that identifies you.

Children

Fetchly does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
