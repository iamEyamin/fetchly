Help and Support

Can't find your answer? Email smpleyamin@gmail.com and include your app version (Settings › About Fetchly).

Where are my downloads?

In the Files tab of the app, and in Apple's Files app under On My iPhone › Fetchly. Hidden files appear only inside Fetchly, behind the eye button in the Files tab.

Why did a download stop when I closed the app?

Downloads keep going when you switch to another app or lock the phone. If you swipe Fetchly away in the app switcher, iOS stops them. Open Fetchly again and they continue from where they stopped.

Why can't I save videos from some sites?

Fetchly does not list or download media on sites whose terms forbid saving their content, such as YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and subscription streaming services. Copy-protected (DRM) streams cannot be saved from any site.

A website looks broken

The ad blocker may be hiding something the page needs. In the Browser tab, open the ⋯ menu and choose "Turn ad blocker off for this site".

A download link expired

Long-press the download and choose "Refresh link in browser". Open the page again, start the download, and Fetchly continues the same file from the new link, keeping the part already saved.

A saved stream won't play in the preview

Streams are saved as .ts files, which the built-in preview does not play. Open the file in a player such as VLC or Infuse using the Share button.

How do I schedule a download?

When adding a link, turn on "Start later" and choose a time. iOS starts it around that time, even if the app is closed, and may delay it on low battery or without Wi‑Fi.

Report a bug or suggest a feature

Email smpleyamin@gmail.com. For a bug, say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.
